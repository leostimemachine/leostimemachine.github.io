Privacy Policy – Leo Studio

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Leo Studio (“we”, “our”, or “the application”) is a personal automation tool that allows the owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize Leo Studio, the application requests access to the following data through Google OAuth:

  • Permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload scope)
  • Permission to view basic information about your YouTube channel and its videos (youtube.readonly scope), used only to show which channel is connected and to confirm that each upload is complete

The application does not access your Google account password, email content, contacts, or any other Google services beyond the YouTube scopes listed above.

2. How We Use the Information

The data is used exclusively to upload video files to your own YouTube channel and set title, description, visibility, schedule and thumbnails as instructed by you.

3. Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer only. No video files or personal data are uploaded to any external server controlled by Leo Studio.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. The only entity that receives data is Google/YouTube, as required to perform the upload operations.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens remain stored locally until you revoke access from your Google Account settings.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke Leo Studio’s access at any time from your Google Account under Security → Third-party access.

8. YouTube API Services and Google

  • Leo Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Leo Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
  • Google’s privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy
  • You can revoke Leo Studio’s access to your data at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
  • Leo Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Leo Studio does not use cookies and does not let third parties serve content or advertisements. On your device it stores only the OAuth token (section 3) and, in its own project files, the ID and status of each video it uploaded, so that the owner can find it in YouTube Studio.
  • Leo Studio is used only by the adult owner of the channel. It does not collect any information from children or from the channel’s viewers.
