The production tool behind Leo's Time Machine

Leo Studio is a private desktop app for macOS. Its only user is the creator of the YouTube channel Leo's Time Machine, an educational history channel for children aged 4–8.

What Leo Studio does

Every week the creator makes new episodes and Shorts about one period of history. Leo Studio helps put each video together and then uploads it to the creator's own channel, with its title, description, tags, custom thumbnail, “made for kids” setting and scheduled publish time.

Leo Studio is not sold, distributed or offered to anyone else.

How Leo Studio uses your Google Account

Leo Studio uses YouTube API Services. When the channel owner connects the channel, Leo Studio asks Google for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, “made for kids” setting and publish time.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel is connected and to check that each upload finished and is scheduled.

Leo Studio does not read, store or share any other Google data, and it never accesses anyone else's channel.

Your data stays on your computer

The sign-in token is saved only on the owner's Mac. Leo Studio has no servers, no user accounts, no ads, no cookies and no analytics. Access can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page.
